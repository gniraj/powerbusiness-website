Antenna

VHF/UHF antenna on the roof, coax to the rack. Captures every free-to-air broadcast in range.

  • Site survey & signal check before install
  • Amplifier / splitter as required
  • One-time channel scan & logical numbering
📻

ATSC receiver

Multi-tuner box that demodulates the broadcast and outputs each channel as an MPEG-TS multicast stream.

  • 8- or 16-channel units, 1U rack
  • UDP/RTP multicast out, one group per channel
  • ATSC 1.0 today, ATSC 3.0-ready models available
🖥️

IPTV server & gateway

Ingests the receiver's multicast, repackages it as HLS, and publishes the channel lineup, EPG and playlist every TV reads.

  • Adds IPTV provider feeds, satellite and a hotel channel
  • Channel API, M3U playlist, programme guide, catch-up
  • Appliance or virtual server on the hotel LAN; UDP, HLS, SRT, RTSP
🔀

Core & floor switches

Managed switches carry the streams to every floor without flooding the network.

  • IGMP snooping & querier on the core, 1 Gbps backbone
  • Dedicated TV VLAN, QoS for video
  • PoE floor switches for PoE boxes; Wi-Fi 6 for retrofits
📺

Google-certified Android TV / Google TV box behind each TV, running the PowerBusiness launcher in kiosk mode.

FAQ

Questions hotels ask us

How long does installation take?

A typical 100-room property is live in two to three days after the site survey: one day for the headend and network, one to two for boxes and provisioning. Branding is done from the manager app and can start before hardware arrives.

Do we need to replace our TVs?

No. Any TV with an HDMI input works. Existing hospitality TVs (Samsung, LG, Philips, Vestel) keep their Pro:Idiom / LYNK tuning; the box simply becomes the TV's home screen.

What network do we need?

A 1 Gbps backbone with IGMP snooping for multicast is ideal. Wi-Fi 6 works for retrofits; the boxes receive HLS unicast, so no multicast is required to the room.

Can guests use AirPlay and Chromecast?

Yes. Each TV appears as "Room 1204 TV" on the guest Wi-Fi. Isolation keeps guests from seeing other rooms' TVs; sessions clear at checkout.

Does it work without internet?

Antenna channels, the hotel channel, the launcher and the manager app all run on the local network. Internet is needed only for streaming apps, IPTV provider feeds and licensed VOD.

Which protocols does the headend support?

UDP/RTP multicast in from receivers; HLS out to devices; SRT and RTSP sources; M3U playlists and an EPG API for any IPTV client.

Can we buy the software only?

Yes. If you already have a headend and network, we license the launcher, control server and manager app per room and integrate with your existing streams.

How does PMS integration work?

Check-in creates the guest on the TV (name, language, checkout); charges post to the folio; checkout wipes the room's sessions and resets the TV to Welcome. Standard PMS interfaces (e.g. Opera, Mews, Cloudbeds) or a simple REST bridge.
